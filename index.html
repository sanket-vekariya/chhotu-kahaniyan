Chhotu Kahaniyan

Short animated money stories for Indian families, in Hindi and Hinglish.

Chhotu is a curious boy who learns about money from his family's own past mistakes: Papa's job switch, Mummy's UPI "ting", Dadaji's old cloth potli of notes. Each episode explains one personal-finance idea from a well-known book (budgeting, saving, the pain of paying, real hourly wage and more) in under two minutes, with real numbers worked out on screen.

Paise ki Pathshala: for learning only, not financial advice.
