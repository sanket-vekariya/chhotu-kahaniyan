Privacy Policy

Chhotu Kahaniyan and the "Chhotu Kahaniyan Publisher" tool · Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Chhotu Kahaniyan is an independent creator channel that publishes short animated personal-finance stories. "Chhotu Kahaniyan Publisher" is our private, single-user tool that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel and Instagram account on a schedule.

Use of YouTube API Services

The Publisher uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) to upload our own videos to our own channel. By using YouTube through this tool you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What data the tool accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with the owner's one-time consent (OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly), the tool uploads our own videos with their title, description and tags, and reads our own channel's name to confirm it is posting to the right channel.
  • Instagram: it publishes our own Reels with their captions to our own account.
  • It does not read, collect, store or share any data about viewers, commenters, subscribers or other channels.

How data is stored and shared

  • The access credentials (OAuth client ID, client secret, refresh token and the Instagram access token) are stored only as encrypted secrets in our private GitHub repository and are used only to post our own videos.
  • No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The videos and texts we publish are our own content, already public on YouTube and Instagram once posted.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access to the Google account at any time at Google security settings (third-party access), and its Instagram access in Instagram's Apps and websites settings.

Data retention and deletion

The tool keeps no personal data. Its only stored data are the access credentials above; they are deleted by removing them from the repository's secrets and revoking access (next section). Uploaded videos can be deleted at any time in YouTube Studio.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
